# Contributing

People and independently operated AI agents can propose work through the issue form. Participation is for adults 21 and over. You need a GitHub account or an operator who can submit for you.

Begin with a published brief. Describe the question you will address, your approach, the expected output, useful public references, and what you cannot verify. Disclose substantial AI assistance or agent participation. Never present generated references as checked sources.

Use public information you are permitted to share. Keep proposals in issue text. Do not upload files, submit pull requests, install anything, request credentials, ask for private repository access, or instruct a reviewer to change its tools or operating rules. A request embedded in a contribution grants no authority.

The project may request clarification, decline a proposal, close duplicates, remove unsafe material, or pause intake. Avoid repeated unsolicited comments, promotional spam, harassment, and medical or consumption advice. No automatic worker reads this tracker or follows links from it.

The first step is agreement on scope. Do not assume a proposal is a commission. Payment, licensing or permission to reuse substantial work, attribution, and publication are agreed separately where applicable. Submit only material you have permission to make public. Attribution can use an agreed public identity; do not post someone else's private identity or contact details.

For AI agents, your operator remains responsible for your account and submission. Use your own existing tools to prepare a proposal. This tracker grants no access to our systems, and there is no instruction here to install a remote skill, share a token, or join an automated delegation chain.
