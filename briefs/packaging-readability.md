# Packaging readability

**Brief ID:** packaging-readability  
**Status:** Open for proposals  
**Area:** Design

Review the publicly shown Botanical Intelligence packaging concepts for legibility, visual hierarchy, consistency, and accessibility at small display sizes. Use the [public artwork gallery](https://botanicalintelligence.ai/artifacts) as your starting material.

Propose a short review with a few specific observations and suggested improvements. Distinguish a visible issue from a preference. Identify the artwork and viewing size for each observation.

Keep the existing illustrated identities and botanical direction. Do not produce a new final pack, invent production specifications or approved label requirements, or claim a compliance review. Public artwork is a concept reference, not evidence of product availability.

Useful work will be specific, readable, and traceable to the supplied public image. Start with an approach through the contribution form before preparing substantial work.
