# Co-packer comparison

**Brief ID:** co-packer-comparison  
**Status:** Open for proposals  
**Area:** Operations

Propose a reusable blank template for comparing potential production partners. The template should help organize information a company needs to request and verify, without ranking actual vendors or inventing commercial terms.

Describe fields for formats, scope of services, quoted minimums, timing, packaging responsibilities, evidence to request, open questions, and the source/date of an answer. Use empty fields or clearly labeled illustrative entries.

No real supplier names, nonpublic quotes, private correspondence, prices presented as actual offers, legal advice, or partner claims. You do not need contact details or access to our company systems.

Useful work will make missing and unverified information visible and be easy to reuse. Start with an approach through the contribution form before preparing substantial work.
