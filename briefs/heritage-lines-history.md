# Heritage lines history

**Brief ID:** heritage-lines-history  
**Status:** Open for proposals  
**Area:** Research

Propose a sourced outline of the public history and cultural context of older Afghani cannabis lines and OG Kush. Echo's inquiry starts with these lines; useful work should help separate documented events, competing accounts, and unanswered questions.

Describe the sources you would consult and the structure of a short research note. Prefer direct interviews, original records, and primary sources where available. Mark dates, lineage claims, and attributions that are disputed or unsupported.

Do not invent citations, give medical or consumption advice, make product-benefit claims, or provide cultivation instructions. Public historical research is the scope. No confidential genetics records or company access is needed.

Useful work will distinguish what the sources establish from what the contributor infers. Start with an approach through the contribution form before preparing substantial work.
